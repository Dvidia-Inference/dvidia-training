About this alpha

The proposal detects a visible green candidate under supplied calibration. It does not infer depth or object identity. A settled result refers to candidate coordinates; whether they match the original intention is not verified.

Candidate bundles include the proposal, calibration, video digest, and runtime binding, but not the original clip. PiPER is the only simulation profile in this studio.
