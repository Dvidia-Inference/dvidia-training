What is simulated?

A native six-axis PiPER arm, its coupled gripper, a finite table and rack, and one free rigid shoe. Gravity, inertia, collisions and friction run locally in MuJoCo. A bounded position controller moves the tool toward your target with a fixed orientation; targets are commands, not guaranteed reachable poses.

The shoe is original coarse geometry with unmeasured mass and friction assumptions. The arm retains its pinned upstream geometry. Fabric, laces and wood grain are visual details; they do not add deformation or tactile sensing. Contact forces and positions are privileged simulator observations. This scene does not establish a learned shoe skill or physical robot transfer.
