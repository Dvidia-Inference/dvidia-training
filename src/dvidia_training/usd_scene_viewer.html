What this proves

The bridge reads a deliberately small USD scene: a rigid object, a tabletop and explicit physical properties. The native simulator keeps the pinned robot mechanics. This experiment checks falling, resting contact and sliding across mass, friction and height variations. Removing gravity, support or friction supplies negative controls.

The browser replays saved native body poses. It does not run physics, train a policy or connect to a robot. Properties remain assumptions until measured on real objects. No shoe grasp, video-to-action learning or physical transfer is qualified by these checks.
