A scene is more than its appearance.

The recipe describes dimensions, poses, mass and friction alongside a pinned arm profile. Native simulation checks the resulting contacts and motion. Drafts with invalid geometry are rejected before a run.

These checks help build reusable training environments. They do not establish real-object calibration, deformable shoe mechanics, learned grasping or physical robot transfer.

Keep it on your machine.

Use the same recipe from the editor or the Terminal. Once the Python environment and pinned robot assets are installed, local simulation runs on the CPU.

dvidia-environment studio \
  --assets ./robot-assets --output ./local-scenes --port 8346

The browser only draws geometry and saved poses. “Validate & simulate” is available when served by the local Python application.
