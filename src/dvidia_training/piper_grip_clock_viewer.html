Report · Protocol · Frozen references

The frame timeline follows fixed physical simulation time. Clock progress and wait debt are separate recorded outputs. “Clock output” is the model's advance probability, not a safety confidence. Terminal frames have no invented next action. Failed attempts remain included, including outcomes with no saved native frames. Playback uses saved states and does not run new physics or inference. The complete source evidence is linked below.

All seven arm and jaw references are frozen. The clock without grip measurements keeps the same starting scene, time features and movement limits. Every simulation uses the same 20 ms control interval. The selected variant chooses whether virtual progress holds or advances. No camera or physical robot has been tested here.
