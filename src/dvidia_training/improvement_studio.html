        From video to a robot skill

        The development path: a video supplies evidence. Action data, a calibrated environment and tests turn that evidence into a bounded robot capability.

        1. Understand the demonstration

          Review timed actions, objects and state changes. Retain uncertainty, failures and recovery.

        2. Connect observations to actions

          Synchronize camera observations with measured robot or instrumented-gripper commands. Ordinary phone video does not supply these commands.

        3. Build the task in simulation

          Calibrate the robot, objects, contacts and sensors. Define the goal, limits and independent success checks.

        4. Test beyond the training examples

          Use held-out conditions, recovery and fault tests. Cover a declared operating range; unseen situations remain unknown.

        5. Validate on the physical robot

          Check the specific hardware under supervision before releasing a skill with supported conditions and known limits.

        No finite simulator covers every possibility. More videos help only when they supply useful missing evidence.
